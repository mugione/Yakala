```
Yakala (Turkish for "capture") is a fast, focused screenshot tool that lives in your menu bar. Capture, annotate, share — in seconds.

CAPTURE
• Press ⇧⌘2 to freeze the screen and drag to select an area; a single click takes the whole screen.
• Press ⇧⌘1 and click a window to capture it with transparent rounded corners.
• Multiple displays and Retina resolution are supported.

ANNOTATE
• Arrow, rectangle, ellipse, pen and highlighter.
• Text and colored labels that stay readable on busy screens.
• Numbered steps (①②③), ✓ ✗ ? ! stamps and a 2× magnifier.
• Blur or pixelate passwords, emails and personal details in a second.
• Every shape can be selected, moved and recolored later; full undo and redo.

BEAUTIFY
• Place your screenshot on a colorful background in one click.
• 8 backgrounds, padding, corners, shadow, 16:9 / 4:3 / 1:1 ratios and a window frame.
• Your last settings are remembered — ready for slides and social posts.

SHARE
• ⌘C copies, closes the editor and takes you back to your app — just paste.
• ⌘S saves; optionally every capture is saved to your folder automatically.
• Quick mode: copy or save instantly without opening the editor.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No network access, no data collection, no ads, no tracking.
• Your screenshots never leave your Mac.
• Open source: the full code is on GitHub.

The interface is in Turkish. Shortcuts can be changed in Settings. Requires macOS 14 Sonoma or later.
```

## İnceleme notları (App Review Information › Notes)

```
Yakala is a menu bar app (LSUIElement) and has no Dock icon. After launch, a welcome window explains the shortcuts.

How to test:
1. Click the Yakala icon in the menu bar, or press Shift-Command-2, then drag to select an area.
2. Grant Screen Recording permission when macOS asks (System Settings › Privacy & Security › Screen & System Audio Recording), then relaunch Yakala.
3. The editor opens: draw arrows, text, blur; click "Güzelleştir" (Beautify); press Command-C to copy.
Shift-Command-1 captures a single window. Right-click the menu bar icon for the menu (Settings, About, Quit).

The app does not use the network and collects no data. No account or login is needed. The interface is in Turkish.
```
